## Purpose
Capture applicable reviewer lessons and convert them into bounded design, evaluation, reporting, or claim safeguards without allowing reviewer feedback to silently change frozen science.

## Current safeguards
- separate mechanism evidence from performance claims;
- report trade-offs and limitations rather than only favorable metrics;
- preserve baseline comparability and provenance;
- avoid convergence, optimality, and universality claims not established by evidence;
- keep canonical AHBN immutable while evaluating Q-AHBN2.
